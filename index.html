🌱 About EcoAura

🌍 Our Mission

Our mission is to create beautiful jewellery while encouraging creativity, reusing materials, and caring for our environment.

EcoAura is our Class 9-D school project. We create beautiful handmade jewellery using eco-friendly ideas. Our goal is to show that creativity and care for nature can go together.
